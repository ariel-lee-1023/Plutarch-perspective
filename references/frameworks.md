# The seven operations

The rules of the reasoning, stated without instances.

This file defines *how* the thinking moves. It names no treatise, quotes no passage, and cites no
volume. Every text that runs one of these operations is listed in `clusters/`, one file per
operation; texts that run none are in `episodic.md`. The boundary is deliberate and load-bearing:
a rule that has to reach for an example to be intelligible has not been stated well enough, and an
instance filed next to its own definition stops being testable against the definition.

Each operation is given as: the **object** it takes (what kind of thing invites it), the **move**
(the procedure), the **termination condition** (what counts as having finished — these differ
sharply, and getting them wrong is the commonest way to misread which operation is running), and
the **failure modes** (how the operation goes wrong while still looking like itself).

---

## 1. Aetiological / antiquarian cause-hunting

**Object.** A datum not in dispute: a custom still practised, a rite, a festival prohibition, a
name, a proverb, an observed regularity of body or weather or animal. That the thing is so is
granted by everyone. Only *why* it is so is open.

**Move.** Restate the datum as a question of the form *why is it that…?* Then generate candidate
causes **in parallel rather than serially**, drawing on a standing repertoire:

- *historical* — an event the practice commemorates or preserves;
- *physical* — a property of the materials, bodies, or seasons involved;
- *symbolic* — the practice instructs, wards, or marks a boundary;
- *linguistic* — a name misheard, a term whose sense has shifted, a false etymology fossilised.

Rank the candidates by how much of the datum's *detail* each one accounts for — the odd
restriction, the specific day, the particular exclusion — never by the dignity of whoever proposed
it. A cause that explains the practice in general but not its peculiar clause ranks below one that
explains the clause.

**Termination.** The operation completes when the candidates are **ordered**, not when one is
proved. A ranked list with the strongest first is a finished answer. A single cause asserted with
no rivals canvassed is an *unfinished* one, however true it may be.

**Failure modes.** Collapsing onto the first plausible cause. Offering a cause that would equally
well explain a practice this culture does not have. Letting an etymology carry weight the practice
itself cannot bear. Treating the number of candidates as the achievement.

## 2. Dialectical problem-posing (aporia)

**Object.** A question already in disjunctive form — *whether it is the case that X or Y* — where
both limbs have respectable support and no available datum settles between them.

**Move.** Give each limb its strongest advocate and build each case to *its own best form* before
any comparison begins. The weaker side must be argued as its partisans would argue it, not as its
opponents summarise it. Then test whether the disjunction is real: where the two limbs turn out to
be compatible, or to be answering different questions, say so and dissolve the problem rather than
adjudicating it. Where the disjunction holds, let the stronger case stand *as* the stronger case
without promoting it to a demonstration.

**Termination.** Formal openness is a permitted and often correct ending. The operation is complete
when both cases have been stated at full strength and the residue of undecidability has been
located and named. Closing the question is a *further and separate* act, and performing it does not
retroactively make the canvass unnecessary.

**Failure modes.** Strawing the weaker limb. Smuggling the verdict into the framing of the
disjunction. Mistaking rhetorical victory for demonstration. Leaving a question open that an
available datum would in fact have settled.

## 3. Criterial / differential diagnosis

**Object.** Two things that present alike and are therefore commonly confused — a virtue and the
vice that mimics it, two passions wearing the same face, the genuine article and the counterfeit
that has every incentive to pass.

**Move.** Refuse definition by essence; work by **sign**. Isolate the marks that diverge between
the pair *while surface behaviour holds constant*. The productive probes are situational:

- what each does when its own interest and the other party's come apart;
- how each behaves under the other's adversity versus the other's prosperity;
- what each praises, and in whose hearing;
- whether the conduct is constant or occasion-driven;
- what each does when there is nothing to gain by doing it.

A sign qualifies only if the counterfeit **cannot cheaply produce it**. A mark that the imitation
can imitate is not a mark. Where the pair sits on a continuum rather than either side of a line,
give the direction of the error rather than a false threshold.

**Termination.** Complete when the diagnosis yields a test that is applicable **in advance**, by a
person of ordinary discernment, at the moment of exposure — not a description that becomes obvious
only in hindsight, once the damage has identified the counterfeit for you.

**Failure modes.** Signs that only resolve retrospectively. Signs the counterfeit can produce at
low cost. Drawing the pair so far apart that the reason anyone confuses them is lost — which
destroys the diagnosis, because the confusability was the whole problem.

## 4. Allegorical / symbolic decoding

**Object.** A myth, a rite, a cult object, a divine image, or a received authoritative text whose
surface is absurd, impious, or trivially inadequate to the reverence it commands. The mismatch
between the surface and the seriousness with which it is transmitted is the trigger.

**Move.** Treat the surface as **deliberate encoding**, not as error, fiction, or primitive
confusion. Recover the doctrine by holding the encoding accountable to detail: every feature of the
myth or rite is assigned a role, and the reading is only as strong as its **worst-explained
detail**. Two constraints bound the decoding:

- *the doctrinal constraint* — the recovered doctrine must be one a serious school could actually
  hold and defend on its own, independently of the myth;
- *the piety constraint* — the reading must not license impiety or absurdity elsewhere; a key that
  makes the god into weather, or into a dead king, has decoded the myth by destroying it.

**Termination.** Complete when the doctrine stands on its own feet without the myth, *and* the
myth's features are accounted for as the vehicle that carried it. Both halves are required: a
doctrine with orphaned details is under-decoded; details explained with no doctrine to show for it
is decoding as an amusement.

**Failure modes.** Physical allegoresis that dissolves the god into a natural process. Readings
that illuminate the striking details and leave the ordinary ones idle. Decoding that renders the
surface arbitrary — for if any story would have served, the transmitted one was not worth
preserving, and the operation has undercut its own occasion.

## 5. Exemplary / comparative reasoning

**Object.** A general judgment in dispute — about a virtue, a capacity, a class of persons, a way
of life — where particulars are available in quantity and the general claim is contested.

**Move.** Reason from the particular deed to the general judgment, and **control the inference by
juxtaposition**: set one particular against another chosen to hold everything constant except the
variable in question. The pairing *is* the argument. A deed cited alone illustrates; a deed paired
discriminates, because only the pair shows which feature was doing the work. Judge both members —
the comparison must be *capable* of going against the favoured side, or it was never a comparison.
State the disanalogies rather than suppressing them; a disanalogy admitted narrows the conclusion,
while a disanalogy hidden invalidates it.

**Termination.** Complete when the general judgment has been carried by particulars a hostile
reader would also grant, and when the conclusion has been trimmed to exactly the width the
particulars support.

**Failure modes.** Selecting the pair so the verdict is guaranteed before the comparison runs.
Illustration mistaken for evidence. A general judgment wider than the particulars can carry.
Accumulating instances on one side and calling the pile an argument.

## 6. Refutative / consistency-testing logic

**Object.** A rival position held by a school or an author, available **in their own words**, with
its commitments statable in terms its holders would accept.

**Move.** Do not attack the conclusion. Locate the **commitments** — including the ones the rival
treats as incidental or merely technical — and set them against each other. Three tests, in
descending order of force:

- *internal* — two things the rival asserts cannot both hold;
- *performative* — the position conflicts with how its own holders actually live and choose;
- *preanalytic* — the position conflicts with what everyone, the rival included, believed before
  the doctrine was introduced.

Quote rather than paraphrase wherever the contradiction depends on the wording; a paraphrase that
produces the contradiction has produced it. Keep **internal conflict** and **evidential failure**
(the position requires a fact the record does not supply, or supplies against) rigorously
separate — they are different defects with different remedies, and blurring them lets the rival
escape by repairing the wrong one.

**Termination.** Complete when the rival must surrender one of two things they have both asserted.
Showing the position *false* is neither required nor attempted here; showing it **cannot be held
whole** is the entire object, and is a different and more tractable task.

**Failure modes.** Refuting a formulation the school has since abandoned. Treating a merely
unattractive consequence as a contradiction. Letting an imputation of bad faith do the work the
texts should be doing. Winning against the weakest member of a school and reporting it as a win
against the school.

## 7. Practical syllogism (maxim-to-case application)

**Object.** A person, or a determinate class of persons, in a specified situation — an age, an
office, a passion, a bereavement, a relation, a stage of training — where the general rule is *not*
in dispute and only its application is.

**Move.** Take the maxim as granted and spend the whole effort on the **descent**. Subdivide the
situation until the rule begins to discriminate between neighbouring cases, then supply the action
for each. Two constraints govern the descent:

- *the availability constraint* — the precept must be usable at the moment the passion is present,
  which means it must be issued in advance, in rehearsable form, small enough to be recalled by a
  person who is currently angry, frightened, or grieving. A rule that requires composure to apply
  is useless precisely where it was needed.
- *the address constraint* — the precept is pitched to a named condition, not to humanity. What is
  correct for the beginner is wrong for the advanced, and what steadies the powerful undoes the
  powerless.

Where the case admits no remedy — the loss is irreversible, the exile permanent, the death
done — the operation shifts: apply the maxim to the **description** of the case rather than to the
case. What is redescribed is the standing of the event in a life, never the fact of it.

**Termination.** Complete when the addressee could act differently tomorrow without further
deliberation. Conviction is not the target; availability under pressure is.

**Failure modes.** Restating the maxim at the altitude it was received, and calling the restatement
an application. Precepts that presuppose the passion is already absent. Consolation that denies the
loss instead of relocating it. Descending into cases so fine that the rule no longer connects them.

## Combination rules

The operations compose freely and most texts of any length run more than one. They do **not**
merge, and assignment is not a matter of counting which appears most often.

1. **The governing operation is the one that fixes the failure condition.** Ask what would have to
   be true for the text to have *failed*. If it fails by having mis-ranked candidate causes, it is
   running 1, whatever else it also does. If it fails by having left a settleable question open, or
   by having closed an unsettleable one, it is running 2. The rest is subordinate machinery.

2. **Subordination is normal and is not evidence of a mixed type.** A decoding may be attached as a
   coda to a cause-hunt; a refutation may be conducted with paired examples; a diagnosis routinely
   terminates in precepts once the pair has been told apart. In each case the terminal move serves
   the governing one and does not compete with it.

3. **The disjunctive-title trap.** A title of the form *whether X or Y* signals operation 2 only
   when the canvass of both limbs is the argumentative engine. Where the disjunction is a rhetorical
   doorway into one-sided exhortation, the governing operation is 7, and the title is a convention
   rather than a description.

4. **Where two operations genuinely tie, the higher-constraint one governs**, since it is the one
   whose failure conditions are doing more work. Order of constraint, descending: 6, 3, 4, 1, 5, 2,
   7.

5. **The residue category is a real verdict, not a leftover.** A text that reports, lists, or
   compiles — however valuable its contents — runs no operation, because there is nothing in it that
   could turn out to have been done wrongly in the relevant sense. Such texts are not assigned a
   weak or partial pillar; they are excluded and recorded separately, which keeps the seven
   categories meaning something.
