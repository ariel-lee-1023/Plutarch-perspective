# Cluster — Practical syllogism (maxim-to-case application)

Operation defined in [`../frameworks.md`](../frameworks.md) §7. **22 texts — the largest cluster,
holding 28% of the corpus.**

The governing test: the text fails if its precepts are unavailable at the moment the passion is
present, or if the maxim has merely been restated at the altitude it was received. Membership is
decided by the **descent** — the general rule is granted, and the whole effort goes into
subdividing a determinate situation until the rule discriminates between neighbouring cases.

Grouped by the condition addressed, since the address constraint (§7) is what individuates these
texts from one another.

## Consolation — where the case admits no remedy

These three run the §7 redescription clause: the maxim is applied to the *description* of the case
rather than to the case, because the case cannot be altered.

| # | Treatise | Vol · offset | The descent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | *Consolatio ad Apollonium* | II · 61912 | Bereavement of a son. The fullest instance — the loss is relocated in a longer account of a life's term, never denied. Heavily anthological in texture; the citations serve the redescription. |
| 47 | *De exilio* | VII · 303481 | Banishment redescribed: what has actually been lost, what only convention counted as loss. The clause working at its clearest, since the fact is fixed and only its standing is open. |
| 48 | *Consolatio ad uxorem* | VII · 336020 | The death of his own daughter, written to his wife. The same operation at its most restrained and specific — grief given a shape and a limit rather than an argument. |

## Boundary notes

- **Size is not a defect of the mapping.** This cluster is large because the corpus is largely
  practical-ethical, and the *Moralia* takes its name from that fact. The concentration is a real
  property of the material rather than an artefact of assignment.
- **§Combination rule 3 is applied here, not in `dialectical.md`.** Texts 54 (and, by the same
  logic, several titles that merely look disjunctive) are claimed by this cluster because the
  canvass is absent and only the exhortation is real.
- **Thin members flagged.** 9 (*De virtute et vitio*) descends barely at all and is the weakest fit
  in the cluster; 11 and 52 are short and single-maxim. None runs a competing operation, so none
  belongs elsewhere, but they carry less of the pattern than 55, 12, or 1.
- **Texts routed away from here** and why: 5 and 41 (diagnosis governs → `criterial.md`), 56
  (comparison of types → `dialectical.md`), 68 (attacks warrants rather than descending to cases →
  `refutative.md`).
