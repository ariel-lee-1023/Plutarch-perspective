# Cluster — Exemplary / comparative reasoning

Operation defined in [`../frameworks.md`](../frameworks.md) §5. **5 texts.**

The governing test: the text fails if the general judgment is wider than its particulars can carry,
or if the pairing was rigged before it ran. Note the asymmetry this cluster exposes — the corpus is
saturated with *exempla*, but in most texts they are ornament or evidence in someone else's
argument. Only here does the movement from particular to general judgment carry the text.

| # | Treatise | Vol · offset | The particulars, and the general judgment they carry |
|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | *Mulierum virtutes* — Bravery of Women | III · 226120 | The paradigm, and the only text in the corpus that states the operation in its own preface: the general thesis — that virtue is one and the same in men and in women — is announced, and the deeds are then marshalled to carry it. The §5 juxtaposition rule is explicit: women's deeds are set beside men's *of the same kind*, so that the sex is the only variable left standing. Narrative in texture but argumentative in structure, which is exactly what separates it from the collections in `episodic.md`. |
| 24 | *De Alexandri magni fortuna aut virtute* | IV · 252356 | Deed after deed of Alexander's marshalled to a settled verdict: what he achieved was virtue's work, not luck's. Disjunctive in title, but the disjunction is never really canvassed — the opposing limb exists to be defeated by the particulars. Two declamations, epideictic in register. |
| 35 | *De amore prolis* | VI · 217073 | Particulars drawn from animal behaviour — what beasts endure for their young, unrewarded and untaught — carrying the general judgment that affection for offspring is natural rather than calculated. The control is comparative: animals are chosen precisely because they cannot be suspected of the self-interest the thesis is denying. Survives incomplete. |
| 59 | *Comparationis Aristophanis et Menandri compendium* | X · 317188 | Juxtaposition in its purest form — one poet against another, held to the same standards on diction, characterisation, audience, and moral tendency, yielding a verdict for Menander. Preserved as an epitome; retained here rather than in `episodic.md` because it delivers reasoned judgments that could be wrong. See the genre note in `allegorical.md`. |
| 67 | *Bruta animalia ratione uti* — Gryllus | XII · 256051 | A parade of particular animal behaviours driving the general judgment that beasts possess the virtues — and possess them unlearned, which is the sharper claim. The Circe frame is a device; the engine is the accumulation and pairing of instances against Odysseus' assumption of human pre-eminence. Breaks off unfinished. |

## Boundary notes

- **The split pair, 23 / 24.** *De fortuna Romanorum* and *De Alexandri fortuna aut virtute* share
  a theme, an antithesis, and a register, and are separated here on operation alone: 23's
  antithesis is never resolved and its structure is the contest itself (filed in `dialectical.md`),
  while 24's antithesis is a foil for a verdict the deeds deliver. Anyone loading one should load
  the other; the pairing is genuine even though the operations differ.
- **67 vs 66.** The companion pieces on animal reason divide the same way: *De sollertia animalium*
  is a judged debate left open (operation 2), *Gryllus* is one-sided and particular-driven
  (operation 5). Load together.
- **19 vs the collections.** *Mulierum virtutes* is narrative and anecdotal, and on genre alone
  would sit beside the apophthegm collections in `episodic.md`. It does not, because of its
  preface: the general judgment is stated, and the anecdotes are subordinated to carrying it. This
  is the sharpest test of §Combination rule 5 in the whole mapping — a collection with an argument
  is not a collection.
- **22 is not here.** *Parallela minora* consists entirely of juxtaposed Greek/Roman pairs, which
  is the §5 form. It is in `episodic.md` anyway, because the pairs support no general judgment; the
  juxtaposition is a filing system rather than an inference. A degenerate instance of this
  operation — the mechanism without the conclusion.
- **35's particulars are not deeds.** §5 speaks of deeds, and animal behaviour is not deed in the
  full sense. It is admitted because the inferential structure is unchanged — particulars chosen to
  control a variable, carrying a general claim — and because the choice of animals *is* the
  comparative control.
